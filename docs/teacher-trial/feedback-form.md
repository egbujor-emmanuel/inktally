# REDPEN teacher trial: feedback form

Copy these questions into a Google Form (Forms → Blank form). The question types are in brackets.
Make question 1 required: anyone who doesn't tick it is left out of every result.

---

**1. Consent** [Checkboxes, required]
- [ ] I agree that my answers, timings and corrections can be used, without my name, to describe REDPEN in a competition entry and on its project page.
- [ ] You may also quote me with my first name, subject and the kind of school I teach in (for example "Ada, JSS maths, public school, Enugu"). *(optional)*

I confirm I used student numbers or first names only, and no student faces were photographed. [Checkbox, required]

**2. About you**
- Country and state/region [Short answer]
- Subject(s) and class level(s) you teach [Short answer]
- Usual class size [Number]
- Device you used [Multiple choice: Android phone · iPhone · Laptop/desktop · Tablet], and its model if you know it [Short answer]
- Your usual internet at school [Multiple choice: Good Wi-Fi · Mobile data, mostly fine · Mobile data, often slow or off · Usually none]

**3. What you marked**
- Which way did students answer? [Checkboxes: Printed REDPEN sheet · Exercise book page · Quick mark]
- Type of questions [Checkboxes: Numbers/maths · Multiple choice · One word or short answer · Sentences/theory]
- How many scripts did you mark with REDPEN? [Number]
- Minutes from your first photo to a finished mark register [Number]
- Minutes the same stack usually takes you by hand [Number]
- Did you use it with no data at any point? [Yes / No / I tried and it didn't work]

**4. How accurate was it?**
- How many marks did you have to change in total? [Number]
- Why did you change them? [Checkboxes: It misread the handwriting · The answer key was wrong · It was too strict · It was too lenient · Other]
- Did REDPEN's answer key need fixing before marking? [None · 1–2 answers · Many answers]
- Did the Review tab show the answers you would have wanted to check? [Yes · Mostly · No]

**5. Would you use it?**
- How easy was REDPEN to use? [Linear scale 1–5, 1 = very hard, 5 = very easy]
- How much do you trust the marks? [Linear scale 1–5]
- Would you use REDPEN for your next set of marking? [Yes · Maybe · No]
- Did you share a marked copy or the Excel register with anyone (students, parents, head teacher)? [Yes · No]
- What was the most useful part? [Paragraph]
- What confused you, or didn't work? [Paragraph]
- If you could change one thing, what would it be? [Paragraph]
- Anything else? [Paragraph]
