How to run this test

This pack tests REDPEN end to end on real handwriting at WAEC SSCE standard (also first-year A-level physics): objective questions, structured questions with sub-parts like 6(b)(ii), calculations with units and standard form, method marks for wrong answers, and theory answers marked point by point. We know the correct mark for every answer, so every difference shows.

  1. Find 4 people (different handwriting is the point). Give each person one script page: Script 1, 2, 3 or 4.
  2. Each person copies their script by hand, in pen, onto one clean exercise-book page, in their normal handwriting: the question label first (1, 2 … 6(a), 6(b)(i) …), then the answer exactly as printed, mistakes included. If they run out of space, they may use small writing, but keep it to one page.
  3. At the top they write only “Script 1” (or 2, 3, 4). No real names.
  4. Photograph each page flat, in good light, one page per photo.
  5. Put the photos in Google Drive, in a folder called REDPEN real scripts, and tell me.
Then: I create the assignment from the marking guide, mark all four scripts with REDPEN and compare every mark with the correct one. That run is also our demo footage.

Keep the separate “truth” file to yourself. Don’t give it to the writers.

Physics — WAEC standard

22 marks. Answer all questions. Show your working in Section B. Take g = 10 m/s².

Section A — Objectives (1 mark each)

1. The SI unit of power is:
A. joule   B. watt   C. newton   D. pascal
2. Which of the following is a vector quantity?
A. mass   B. speed   C. displacement   D. energy
3. The image formed by a plane mirror is:
A. real and inverted   B. virtual and upright   C. real and upright   D. virtual and inverted
4. A body moving in a circle at constant speed has:
A. no acceleration   B. an acceleration towards the centre   C. an acceleration away from the centre   D. a constant velocity
5. Which of these is NOT an electromagnetic wave?
A. X-rays   B. radio waves   C. sound waves   D. light

Section B — Structured questions

6. (a) Define acceleration.1 mark
(b) A car starts from rest and reaches a velocity of 20 m/s in 5 s. Calculate:
(i) its acceleration;2 marks
(ii) the distance it covers in that time.2 marks
7. (a) State Ohm’s law.2 marks
(b) Resistors of 4 Ω and 2 Ω are connected in series to a 12 V battery. Calculate the current in the circuit.2 marks
8. (a) Give two differences between transverse and longitudinal waves.2 marks
(b) A wave has a frequency of 50 Hz and a wavelength of 0.4 m. Calculate its speed.2 marks
9. Calculate the pressure at a depth of 10 m in water. (Density of water = 1000 kg/m³.)2 marks
10. A 2 kg mass is lifted through a height of 5 m. Calculate the potential energy it gains.2 marks
