# Real handwritten test pages

Pages written by hand on paper and photographed with a phone (no real students' names). Used for the demo video and
to test REDPEN on real handwriting, unlike the generated samples one folder up.

## Mixed test page (JSS 2) — write this, photograph it, put the photo here

```
Name: Student 1                         JSS 2 — Mixed test

1. Which gas do plants take in for photosynthesis? (1 mark)
   A oxygen   B carbon dioxide   C nitrogen   D hydrogen
   Answer: B

2. Solve 3x + 7 = 22. Show your working. (2 marks)
   3x = 22 − 7
   3x = 15
   x = 5

3. Simplify 2¾ + 1⅝ (1 mark)
   Answer: 3⅜                         ← wrong on purpose (4⅜)

4. A trader buys 5 bags of rice at ₦18,500 each and sells
   them all for ₦100,000. What is her profit? (2 marks)
   5 × 18,500 = 92,500
   Profit = ₦7,500

5. What is the capital of Kenya? (1 mark)
   Answer: Nairobi

6. Give the past tense of "bring". (1 mark)
   Answer: brang                      ← wrong on purpose (brought)

7. State two functions of the blood. (2 marks)
   It carries oxygen round the body and it fights germs.

8. Explain why ice floats on water. (2 marks)
   Because ice is lighter than water.  ← half right (misses: expands on freezing, so less dense)
```

Expected: **9/12** — Q3 and Q6 wrong, Q7 both points, Q8 one of two points.

## Printed REDPEN sheets (optional — the strongest demo: names stay on the phone, works offline)

Print one of these PDFs (4 copies each, A4, "actual size" — do not "fit to page"). Each student writes only inside the
boxes. Photograph each sheet flat with **all four black squares** in the photo. In REDPEN open the matching sample
(Home → *Try it with a sample*) → **Mark** → photograph or **Choose photos**.

### `science-answer-sheets-x4.pdf` — Basic Science, "Blood and the Liver" (theory answers marked point by point)

Read the questions out or write them on a board:

1. State two functions of blood. (2 marks)
2. State three functions of the liver. (3 marks)
3. Name the process by which green plants make their food. (1 mark)
4. What is the function of the red blood cell? (1 mark)

Suggested answers, in different people's handwriting:

| | Student 1 | Student 2 | Student 3 | Student 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | carries oxygen; fights germs | it helps blood clot and carries food | transports waste | carries oxygen round the body |
| 2 | makes bile; stores glycogen; removes poisons | produces heat | makes bile | stores sugar and makes bile |
| 3 | photosynthesis | fotosynthesis | respiration | photosynthesis |
| 4 | carries oxygen | fights disease ← a listed wrong answer | carries oxygen | transports oxygen |

### `english-answer-sheets-x4.pdf` — English, "Grammar Check"

1. The plural of 'child' is: (A) childs (B) children (C) childrens (D) child's
2. The opposite of 'ancient' is: (A) old (B) aged (C) antique (D) modern
3. She ___ to school every day: (A) goes (B) go (C) going (D) gone
4. The capital of Nigeria is ___
5. Past tense of 'run'

| | Student 1 | Student 2 | Student 3 | Student 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | B | B | A | B |
| 2 | D | **A** ← really writes A | D | D |
| 3 | A | B | A | A |
| 4 | Abuja | FCT | Lagos | Abuja |

Student 2 writing a real "A" on question 2 is the check that REDPEN's learned misreads never change what a student wrote.
