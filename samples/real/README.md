# Real handwritten test pages

Pages written by hand on paper and photographed with a phone (no real students' names). Used for the demo video and
to test REDPEN on real handwriting, unlike the generated samples one folder up.

## Mixed test page (JSS 2) — write this, photograph it, put the photo here

```
Name: Student 1                         JSS 2 — Mixed test

1. Which gas do plants take in for photosynthesis? (1 mark)
   A oxygen   B carbon dioxide   C nitrogen   D hydrogen
   Answer: B

2. Solve 3x + 7 = 22. Show your working. (2 marks)
   3x = 22 − 7
   3x = 15
   x = 5

3. Simplify 2¾ + 1⅝ (1 mark)
   Answer: 3⅜                         ← wrong on purpose (4⅜)

4. A trader buys 5 bags of rice at ₦18,500 each and sells
   them all for ₦100,000. What is her profit? (2 marks)
   5 × 18,500 = 92,500
   Profit = ₦7,500

5. What is the capital of Kenya? (1 mark)
   Answer: Nairobi

6. Give the past tense of "bring". (1 mark)
   Answer: brang                      ← wrong on purpose (brought)

7. State two functions of the blood. (2 marks)
   It carries oxygen round the body and it fights germs.

8. Explain why ice floats on water. (2 marks)
   Because ice is lighter than water.  ← half right (misses: expands on freezing, so less dense)
```

Expected: **9/12** — Q3 and Q6 wrong, Q7 both points, Q8 one of two points.
